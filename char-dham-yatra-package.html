10 Days / 9 Nights Char Dham Yatra Route Plan

Day 1: Dehradun / Haridwar to Barkot

Pickup from Dehradun Railway Station / Airport / Haridwar. Drive to Barkot via Mussoorie & Kempty Falls. Overnight stay in Barkot.

Day 2: Barkot to Yamunotri Dham & Back

Drive to Janki Chatti. Start 6 km trek to Yamunotri Temple. Holy dip in Surya Kund & Darshan. Trek back to Janki Chatti and drive back to Barkot.

Day 3: Barkot to Uttarkashi

Scenic drive along Bhagirathi River to Uttarkashi. Visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple in the evening. Night stay in Uttarkashi.

Day 4: Uttarkashi to Gangotri Dham & Back

Early drive to Gangotri via Harsil Valley. Darshan & Puja at Gangotri Temple. Drive back to Uttarkashi for night stay.

Day 5: Uttarkashi to Guptkashi / Phata

Drive via Tehri Dam and Rudraprayag to Guptkashi / Phata (base for Kedarnath Yatra). Night stay in hotel.

Day 6: Guptkashi to Kedarnath Dham

Drive to Sonprayag/Gaurikund. Trek or take Helicopter to Baba Kedarnath Temple. Evening Aarti & Night stay at Kedarnath Top.

Day 7: Kedarnath to Guptkashi / Pandukeshwar

Morning Darshan at Baba Kedarnath Temple, trek down to Sonprayag. Drive to Guptkashi / Chopta for night rest.

Day 8: Guptkashi to Badrinath Dham

Drive via Joshimath to Badrinath Dham. Holy dip at Tapt Kund, Evening Aarti & Darshan at Badrinath Temple. Night stay in Badrinath.

Day 9: Badrinath (Mana Village) to Rudraprayag

Morning sightseeing at Mana Village (Last Indian Village, Vyas Gufa, Saraswati River). Drive to Rudraprayag / Srinagar for night stay.

Day 10: Rudraprayag to Rishikesh / Dehradun Drop

Drive via Devprayag Sangam & Rishikesh (Ram Jhula / Laxman Jhula). Drop at Dehradun Airport / Railway Station with holy memories.
