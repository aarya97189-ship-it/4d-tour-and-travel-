Kedarnath Yatra 4 Days / 3 Nights Route Plan

Day 1: Dehradun / Haridwar to Guptkashi / Phata

Pickup from Dehradun Railway Station / Jolly Grant Airport / Haridwar. Drive via Rishikesh, Devprayag Sangam, and Rudraprayag to Guptkashi/Phata. Overnight stay in hotel.

Day 2: Guptkashi to Sonprayag / Gaurikund & Trek to Kedarnath Temple

Early morning drive to Sonprayag. Local taxi to Gaurikund. Start the 16 km trek to Baba Kedarnath Temple or take Helicopter/Pony. Attend evening Ganga Aarti & Baba Kedarnath Darshan. Night stay near Temple.

Day 3: Kedarnath Darshan & Trek Down to Guptkashi

Morning Puja and Darshan at Kedarnath Dham. Trek back to Gaurikund / Sonprayag. Drive back to Guptkashi hotel for rest and overnight stay.

Day 4: Guptkashi to Rishikesh / Dehradun Drop

After breakfast, drive back to Dehradun / Haridwar. Sightseeing at Rishikesh Ram Jhula / Laxman Jhula on the way. Drop at Airport / Station.
